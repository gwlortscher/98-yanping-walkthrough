# 98 YANPING

Interactive spatial comparison: Linehouse, Plant inside (v9.5-A), and Plant outside (v9.5-B).

Open the public walkthrough, choose a layout, then select a room to walk inside. Drag to look; use WASD or arrow keys to walk. Dollhouse and Plan views are also available.

Concept reconstruction for discussion with Linehouse. Not a measured survey or engineered design. The source drawings and reconstruction assumptions are included in the viewer.
